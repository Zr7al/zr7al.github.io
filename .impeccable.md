## Design Context

### Users
Rama browsing a personal archive of shared photographs and letters, on phone or desktop. The owner edits its text and captions before publishing.

### Brand Personality
Intimate, candid, restrained. Hope without assumptions about another person's feelings or claims of personal change.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the existing midnight rose and warm daylight themes and typography. A photographic journal with varied, uncropped compositions, handwritten notes, and a quiet final scene. No invented photographs or memories.

### Design Principles
- Give real photographs space and preserve their framing.
- Make every caption and letter easy to personalize in source.
- Keep motion brief and optional; reading never waits on animation.
- Support touch, keyboard, reduced motion, and small screens.
- Preserve static GitHub Pages hosting and transparent notification disclosures.
